## Sequence of events
*Prologue: The village elder has gone up the mountain and has not come back in a few days. The village asked Larry to go up and find out if the elder is okay.*

1. Meet Rosie and you have the following exchange:

    - Rosie: Oh hi Robbie! I got no idea where your Pop Pop went off to, but I'm making MEATBALLS! But I don't have any clean Tupperware!
    - Larry: Alright, well definitely next time, okay?--
    - Rosie: Well--OH! Don't be silly! Now, hold out your hands!

2. Rosie then ladles out a meatball

    - Larry: Ohhhh...cool...thanks a lot!
    - Rosie: Now, please, take a bite so I can watch you enjoy! That's my favorite part!
    - Larry: Will you eat the meatball?
      - Player selects Yes/No
      - If YES then: Rosie: *Giggles with delight* Aren't they great!? Now I just need something to put them in.
      - After saying yes, if you talk to Rosie again, she just says: Do you have any clean Tupperware?
      -  If NO then: Rosie: Okay! Come back when you have a big boy appetite! (the sequence resets back to 1. until you eat the meatball or you present the jar of pickles)

3. Present the Pickle jar to Rosie:

    -  Oh thanks for the Tupperware! I need to get these in the fridge now! You listen to me Robbie Hart, you're going to be a fine husband!
    - Larry: *Confused* ...My name is Larry.
    - Rosie will then leave with her cart, leaving behind a writhing meatball that will slowly squirm away.
    - Rosie, meanwhile, falls over the edge of the waterfall and crashes to the ground with a screen shake and says from the bottom: "I'm fiiiiine!"
    - You cannot interact with the meatball at this point.

4. Once you go up to the mansion and get the shovel when you come back, the meatball will have progressed to stage2 and will be rolling randomly around.

    - The only way to interact with it is to go up and examine it. If you do, you will see Larry say: "<INSERT SHOCKED 80s quote here>"

5. Once you have the sword, you will enter back through the cave and see the meatball has blocked the bridge and is starting to progress to to stage 3. It will have the music and everything and will trigger the fight. You must defeat the meatball to cross the bridge again.

    - The meatball will have two attacks, a throw attack and a smash attack. It will attempt the throw if you stay out of its smash range for too long and will smash if you get too close. It is only vulnerable while wiggling? It can move around but it does so slowly.

6. Game progresses as normal from there.
